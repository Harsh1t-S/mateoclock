Mate O'Clock

A new chess puzzle every day, in under a minute.

Mate O'Clock is a YouTube channel of short chess puzzles. Each Short shows a position, gives you a few seconds to find the winning move, then explains the solution move by move.

Watch on YouTube: @MateOClock

Mate OClock Uploader

Mate OClock Uploader is the channel's own command-line tool. The channel owner runs it on their own computer to upload the finished puzzle videos to the Mate O'Clock channel through the YouTube Data API, as private videos with a scheduled publish time. It asks for one permission (youtube.upload), has no other users, is not offered to anyone else, and reads no YouTube data.

Mate OClock Uploader uses YouTube API Services. The privacy policy explains what data it handles.
